MYPEPS BACKUPS
==============

This is where your backups live.

Each one is named with the date and time it was made:

    MyPeps-Backup-2026-10-05_0849.json
    MyPeps-Backup-2026-10-12_1930.json

Nothing is ever overwritten, so you can go back to any point.


HOW THEY GET HERE

  On a computer (Chrome or Edge)
      Press CHOOSE YOUR ONEDRIVE FOLDER once, pick this folder.
      From then on, BACK UP & FINISH drops a new dated file
      straight in. Nothing to move.

  On a phone
      BACK UP & FINISH opens your share sheet. Choose OneDrive,
      then this folder.


WHAT IS IN A BACKUP
  Stock counts, the full dose history, which vials are open and
  how far through them you are, and any cycles you are tracking.
  One file restores all of it.


TO RESTORE
  Any RESTORE button in the app takes any of these files.
  It also takes an export from the old peptide reminder.
